Key Concepts

  • The class uses a multiple-attendance format allowing students to switch between live online and in-person freely
  • Blender's color-axis convention is blue=Z, red=X, green=Y, but axis direction varies between programs
  • Working with a 2D mouse and 2D screen to manipulate 3D space creates inherent imprecision, so manipulate one dimension at a time
  • Key shortcuts: G (grab/move), R (rotate), S (scale), combined with X/Y/Z to constrain axes, Control to snap, and Shift for fine adjustments
  • The AI policy requires full transparency: instructors and students must disclose any use of AI tools in coursework
  • Object mode manipulates whole objects while Edit mode (Tab) allows direct manipulation of points, edges, and faces—the atomic building blocks of 3D topology
  • Weekly projects are due Tuesdays at 8pm, comprising 75% of the grade, with a final portfolio worth 25%
  • Numpad keys (1, 3, 7, 9) control orthographic camera views, useful for precise 2D-style manipulation within 3D space

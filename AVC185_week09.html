Key Concepts

  • UV seams should follow a 'cardboard construction' mental model—imagine how the object would unfold if built from flat material
  • Avoid excessive seams; use the minimum necessary cuts to let geometry lay flat without distortion
  • Always check for overlapping UV islands, which prevent proper individual texturing of a surface
  • USD (Universal Scene Description) is a non-proprietary format meant to simplify transferring textured models between programs like Substance Painter and Blender
  • Control+Shift+T in Blender with Node Wrangler automatically wires texture folders to shader inputs
  • UV unwrap simple geometry before applying modifiers like Subdivision Surface to avoid added complexity
  • In Substance Painter, fill layers with masks are more flexible and adjustable than direct paint layers
  • Blur and Bevel filters on height-channel masks create smoother, more realistic surface detail

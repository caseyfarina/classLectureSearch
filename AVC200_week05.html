Key Concepts

  • Unity uses the Universal Render Pipeline (URP); mismatched pipeline materials appear pink/magenta as an error
  • A material combines a shader (code) with textures placed on channels like color, normal, and smoothness
  • Diffuse/albedo textures provide base color while normal maps store XYZ surface angle data in RGB channels to fake surface detail
  • Duplicating a material is a quick fix for differing tiling needs, but triplanar shading is a scalable solution for consistent texture scale across objects
  • Triplanar projection maps textures in world space, avoiding stretching regardless of object scale, ideal for walls, floors, and columns
  • UV projection remains necessary for detailed objects like characters, while triplanar suits repeating environmental surfaces
  • Proper texture tiling communicates scale in an environment; mismatched tiling makes spaces feel visually incorrect
  • Particle systems use emitters, lifetime, speed, and size ranges with randomization to create realistic effects like sparkles, fire, or dust; shadows and lighting settings are critical for making interior spaces feel real

Key Concepts

  • Disable trigger zones by default rather than blocking access, then enable them—cleaner logic prevents complexity from compounding later
  • Avoid the 'big bang' approach: build and test modular systems incrementally rather than connecting everything at once and hoping it works
  • Cinemachine allows 'free' camera transitions by simply toggling multiple virtual cameras on/off, with blend time and style configurable in the Cinemachine Brain
  • Respect a 15-minute rule when stuck on a single problem—seek help or move on to other modules rather than wasting limited time
  • Methods (not 'functions') may or may not require arguments; check for input fields to know what data a method needs
  • Glowing particle effects require three components: an emissive material, a post-processing Volume with Bloom, and post-processing enabled on the main camera
  • When parenting a moving particle system to an object, switch Simulation Space from Local to World to avoid unnatural particle motion
  • In Blender, always recalculate normals outside and set the origin point correctly before exporting a door mesh to Unity
  • Maintain a limited, consistent material and color palette for production design instead of using as many materials as possible
  • Optimize presentation video recording by using Maximize on Play, lowering game resolution, closing other heavy programs, and setting OBS to 30fps

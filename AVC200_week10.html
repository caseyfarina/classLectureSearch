Key Concepts

  • Pre-production is about visualizing and pitching an idea as convincingly as possible before implementation
  • Taste, experience, and ideas will matter more than technical button-clicking skills going forward
  • The creative process is nonlinear and full of necessary detours, not a straight path from idea to finished work
  • Oblique strategies encourage going around creative blocks rather than forcing through them
  • Students should reframe their 'meta-game' from passing the class to becoming stronger creative artists
  • Virtual reality environments should be designed intentionally rather than mimicking mundane real-world spaces
  • Substance materials in Unity allow high-fidelity texturing without requiring deep 3D modeling experience
  • Triplanar projection solves texture stretching issues when scaling objects in a 3D environment

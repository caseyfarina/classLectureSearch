Key Concepts

  • A custom geometry node modifier can replicate the inverted hull outline technique in a single reusable step
  • Screen space lines maintain constant thickness regardless of camera distance, unlike world space geometry
  • The Blender asset browser lets you build a personal, local library of reusable meshes, materials, and node groups
  • Marking data as an asset and saving it into your asset library folder makes it instantly draggable into any file
  • Append vs. link vs. pack determine whether imported asset data becomes locally editable
  • Cell shading uses a Shader to RGB node with a color ramp set to constant interpolation for stepped light values
  • Unlit shaders simply feed a color directly into the surface input, ignoring scene lighting
  • Cryptomatte masks allow isolating individual objects in compositing for separate color grading, blur, and stylistic effects

Key Concepts

  • 24fps reads as cinematic due to learned cultural association with film, while 60fps feels like video games or soap operas
  • The Fresnel shader effect is always camera-dependent and cannot be baked into a texture, unlike in Substance Painter
  • Grease pencil line art automatically reorients to whichever camera is active during render
  • Frame interpolation (TV 'soap opera effect') removes motion blur cues and changes the perceived quality of footage
  • Posterizing colors can be done procedurally with snap/quantize nodes on RGB or HSV channels for more flexible toon shading
  • Boosting indirect light in render settings helps fill in overly dark shadows without adding extra lights
  • Cryptomatte allows isolating foreground characters from backgrounds for independent compositing treatments
  • Screen space modifiers keep outline/line thickness consistent regardless of camera distance or world space scale

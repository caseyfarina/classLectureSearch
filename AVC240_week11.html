Key Concepts

  • Shot sizes (extreme close-up through full shot) and camera height form a standardized visual language for communicating framing decisions
  • Pushing in (moving the camera) and zooming in (changing focal length) produce visually distinct effects, especially regarding depth compression
  • Camera stabilization technology (tripods, dollies, steadicams, gimbals, drones) has historically shaped what camera movements are feasible, influencing cinematic style over time
  • Pulling focus keeps a subject sharp as the camera or subject moves, and Blender can automate this using focus tracking on an object
  • Adding noise to key frame constraints like Damped Track can simulate realistic imperfection in otherwise too-perfect computer-generated camera motion
  • Stylized transitions like whip pans and crash zooms function as transitional effects rather than moments meant to be visually processed
  • Reverse-engineering storyboards from existing media requires labeling lensing, shot size, camera angle, camera movement, technique, and lighting for each shot
  • The final music video project should use a song (any genre, with or without lyrics) and be visually compelling without needing to be strictly narrative

Key Concepts

  • The 180-degree rule keeps characters on consistent sides of the screen for spatial coherence
  • Over-the-shoulder shots should prioritize the character being viewed, using proper rule-of-thirds framing
  • Cameras may need per-shot adjustments rather than staying fixed, especially with multiple actions in a scene
  • In Blender Cycles, lower sample counts with denoising and GPU compute drastically reduce render times
  • Rendering at reduced resolution (e.g., 50%) cuts pixel count to a quarter, saving significant render time
  • Shadow flickering issues can often be diagnosed using a process-of-elimination (halving) technique and adjusting shadow pixel allocation or using spotlights instead of point lights
  • Stylistic choices like depth of field and NPR outlines can visually conflict and should be used intentionally
  • Audio-video sync in Blender requires setting frame dropping to stay aligned during playback
